# Code of Conduct

Shellby is a friendly crab, and his corner of GitHub should feel the same way.
This code follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, in shorter words.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or to Claude Code.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas and code, never people.
- Take feedback well, and own your mistakes.
- Keep it on topic and welcoming for everyone, whatever their age, body,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
  education, background, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity
  and orientation.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else you'd reasonably expect to be out of place in a professional space.

## Where it applies

In issues, pull requests, Discussions, the packs gallery, and anywhere you
represent the project.

## Reporting

Report it privately through a
[private advisory](https://github.com/x-salmon/shellby/security/advisories/new)
(start the title with "Conduct:"). Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.
You'll get a reply within a week.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may ask someone to change how they
behave, hide or remove comments, or block someone from the project for a while
or for good. The [Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
are the guide.
