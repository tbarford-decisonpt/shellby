# His tank

Shellby has a home, and you decorate it. Put in a sandcastle, a rock cave, kelp, a treasure chest, the pebble he dug up last Tuesday. He wanders about among it all. Open it from **Shellby → Tank**, or press **Ctrl+K** and type *tank*.

Stickers decorate his shell, and the beach at the top of **Us** shows what you've shipped. The tank is the part you arrange yourself.

## Decorating

Press **Decorate**. While you decorate, he steps to one side and goes see-through, so nothing hides behind him.

- **Put something in:** drag it from the tray into the tank, or click it, or press **Enter** on it. It goes in at the first good spot.
- **Move it:** drag it, or use the arrow keys. **Shift** moves it further. **Up** and **Down** move it a row back or forward.
- **Flip it** with **F**. Use **[** and **]** to send it back or bring it forward among the pieces in its row.
- **Put it away** with **Delete**. It goes back in the tray.
- **Undo and redo** with **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y**, as far back as when you pressed Decorate.
- **Done** keeps it. **Cancel** puts everything back as it was.

The floor has three rows, the back, the middle and the front, and he walks in the middle one. So a castle in the back row stands behind him, and a rock in the front row hides his feet. Plants like kelp stand against the back glass, and the jellyfish lamp floats.

Under the tray you choose the **floor** (sand, gravel, pebbles), the **back glass** (clear water, a rock wall, stars), the **tank size** and the **light**. By default the light follows your clock, so the water darkens at night and lamps and bubblers keep their glow.

Every piece in the tank is a button you can tab to, with its name and where it stands. Screen readers hear what changes as you decorate.

## He lives there

While the Tank is on screen, he uses what you've put in it. Each piece has its own spots:

| Piece | He... |
|---|---|
| Sandcastle Keep, Rock Cave, Sunken Ship, Pile of Leaves | Hides inside, then his eye stalks peek out |
| Rocks, the Sunken Chest, the Guest Bench, the Display Plinth, the Heart Shell | Climbs up and sits |
| Driftwood Arch, Rock Stack, Old Anchor, Beach Ball | Clambers up one side and down the other |
| Rock Cave | Sleeps there at night (when the light is night, or follows the clock and it's dark), with z's |
| Kelp, Java Fern, Moss Ball, Anubias, Cherry Blossom | Nibbles at it |
| Sunken Chest | Checks inside. Nothing, again. He looks at you |
| Amphora, Sunken Ship | Pokes his head out |

- **His favourite** is the piece he uses most. It's named under the tank's title, the Health porthole looks at it, and with motion turned down he sits by it.
- **Something new** gets a look first: after **Done** he goes straight to what you put in, and says something about it on your desktop. The first piece you give him goes in the journal on the **Us** page.
- **Moving day:** when you move him into a bigger tank than he's had before, the pieces hop across one by one and he scuttles in last. It goes in the journal too.
- **Sets on display:** with every find of a set in the tank, the set is on display, named under the title (and in the journal the first time). Take it down later and nothing is lost.
- **On the desktop**, now and then, he says something about his tank: his favourite, a plant he nibbled, a find he moved back. It's rare and follows the chatter setting.

Screen readers hear what he's up to (at most every half a minute).

## Milestones

The rarest pieces each mark a milestone: a hundred bugs in jars, your first bug nest cleared, every Molt, a thriving reef, a review tier, each year of [Shellby Wrapped](WRAPPED.md), levels 25 to 99, the quest chapters, long streaks. Each comes with its own trophy, so it pays trophy XP, celebrates, syncs and gets a "new" dot like any other.

They have their own **Milestones** shelf in the tray. One you haven't earned yet is a dark silhouette with a 🔒 and a ring that fills as you get closer, and says what it takes and how far along you are ("7 / 10 · Clear 10 bug nests"). Once it's yours it gets a gold corner. The ones that move (a bubbling jar shelf, a blinking buoy, falling snow) play in the tray while you point at them, and in the tank all the time. The **Milestones** row under *Where decor comes from* names the one you're closest to.

When you earn one, a toast offers **Put it in the tank**: it opens his tank on the Milestones shelf with that piece picked out.

Levels count the highest you've ever reached, so a Molt never takes a flag down. Secret ones stay secret until they're done.

## Tank sizes

He grows into bigger tanks as he levels up. Each one holds more:

| Tank | Holds | When |
|---|---|---|
| Nano tank | 10 pieces | from the start |
| 10 gallon | 18 | level 5 |
| 30 gallon | 28 | level 15 |
| Reef tank | 40 | level 30, with 10 projects shipped |
| Grand aquarium | 56 | level 50 |

Smaller tanks stay available. If you move to one that holds fewer pieces than you have in, the newest go back in the tray. Moving into a bigger tank than he's ever had is moving day (see above).

## On the Health view

Once there's something in his tank, the picture of him on the **Health** view is a window into it: his floor, his back glass and whatever stands nearest. His moods (sweating, dizzy, stuffed) show on top as before, and reach the water too (see below).

## Gauges in disguise

A few things in his tank quietly show what Shellby already knows about your PC:

| | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Glass Thermometer** (comes with *Check-Up*) | Your GPU's temperature (or the CPU's, with no GPU reading), with a notch at your Health line. It goes red past it. Hang it anywhere in the water; every one you place reads. |
| **Bubblers** (the air stone) | Bubble faster the busier the CPU is. |
| **Lighthouse** | Lit while one of your dev servers is running. It blinks red when one has crashed. |
| **The water** | Takes his Health mood: warm when he's hot (and the plants sway faster), swirling when he's dizzy, a pile of boxes in the corner when he's stuffed. It clears with the mood. |
| **Tide gauge** on the left of the front glass | How much of your 5-hour usage window is left, in tenths. It runs orange below a fifth, and is full again once the window resets. |
| **Sunken Chest** | Glints for a minute when a pull request of yours merges. |
| **Message in a bottle** | Bobs at the surface while a *While you were away* recap or your weekly card is waiting. It sinks once you dismiss the recap or open Trophies & XP. |

Each one has its own switch under **Gauges in disguise** in the Tank, all on to start. They stay on this PC: they never sync and are never on a card. Shellby only tells the tank something changed when it would look different, and only while the panel is open; the tank never asks on a timer.

## Stickers on the glass

Every project in your Sticker Book can go on the tank's front glass, once, from the **Stickers on the glass** shelf of the tray. They're drawn over everything else in the tank. Projects you hid in the Sticker Book don't come up. Stickers on the glass sync with the tank, but never go on your calling card or profile card, since a sticker would name a project.

## Saved layouts

Under **Layouts**, keep up to three tanks by name ("Everyday", "Spooky", "Reef"): **Save the tank as it is**, then **Put up** whichever you like. Saving under a name you already have replaces it. Putting one up, replacing one or removing one can be undone from the toast (or Ctrl+Z), until the tank or the list changes again. A layout goes up like any edit, so a piece you don't have on this PC stays out.

Pick a season for one and it goes up by itself the first time you open his tank once that season has started. When the season's over, the tank you had before comes back. One layout per season. Layouts sync through your private sync gist (the newest change wins); what a season put up stays each PC's own.

## Tidying up

Some days, when you open his tank, he's moved one of his finds a few pixels towards its neighbour. He never moves anything else, never in a tank you changed in the last day, and at most once a day. A note says what he moved: **Put it back** (or **Ctrl+Z**) undoes it, and **Let him tidy up** turns it off. This stays on each PC.

## On your cards, and friends' tanks

His tank is on the **crab card** (📸 Share) once there's something in it: he stands in it, as big as the card allows, with your best stickers on the glass. A wide tank is cropped around its biggest piece.

Your **calling card** (Visiting crabs) and **profile card** are public, so his tank stays off them until you turn on **Show his tank** at the bottom of the Tank. Then:

- Friends see a **Peek at their tank** button next to your name in Settings → GitHub → Visiting crabs. It shows his size of tank, the floor, the back glass, the light and up to 24 pieces, drawn with their own Shellby's art. Specimen jars and decor from packs stay home.
- Your **profile card** shows him standing in it, with the same pieces.
- A friend's crab dropping by while it's on earns **House Guest**, and the Guest Bench.

Turning it off takes his tank off both cards the next time they update, a minute or so later.

With **Sync** on, his tank goes to your other PCs too: whichever PC you decorated on last wins. Whether it's on your cards is up to each PC.

A friend's tank can hold something your Shellby doesn't have yet (from a newer version). It's drawn as a rock, and the peek says how many.

## If a piece goes missing

Pieces from a pack you've removed, or that are locked again because you switched off **Unlock everything**, can't be shown. They keep their place, and the tank says how many there are. Put the pack back and they reappear where they were.

## For developers

- `src/main/tank/index.js`: sizes, rows, the stored tank (`config.tank`), `library` (decor from the wardrobe, plus his finds), `sanitize` and `view`. Pure, and covered in `test/tank.test.js`. It's called `tank`, never `home`: `config.home` is the shell he wears.
- `src/main/tank/life.js`: his life in it, the part main keeps, in its own config key `tankLife` (per PC, never synced or on a card): `uses` per piece and `favourite`, the refs he's already looked at, the biggest tank he's moved into (moving day), the sets he's had on display, and his lines (`reactionLine`, `movingLine`, `remark`, all within the bubble's 24 characters). `afterSave` runs on every `tank:save`. Pure, covered in `test/tank-life.test.js`.
- `src/renderer/panel/tank-life.js`: what he does from moment to moment. `spotsOf` places each piece's spots in the tank, `pick` chooses (new pieces first, bed at night, his favourite three times as often, sometimes just a wander), `pose` says how he looks while he does it (lift, crop, hop, z's), and `moving` lays out moving day. Pure and loaded by the panel and `test/tank-life-pick.test.js`. It only runs inside the Tank tab's own 10 fps loop, so it costs nothing while the tab is hidden; the panel tells main what he used every few activities, or when you leave the tab (`tank:lived`), never every frame.
- `src/main/tank/share.js`: his tank leaving the PC. `forCard` (the calling card's `tank` field, only with `shareCard` on), `cleanCardTank` (a friend's card is untrusted: strict shape, sizes and ranges, at most 24 pieces, refs only built-in decor ids or `find:` ids, so no colours, pixels, URLs or paths ever come from it), `peekView` (their layout with this PC's art; unknown refs become a rock) and `syncable` / `merge` / `applySync` for the sync gist (newer `editedAt` wins; `shareCard` never syncs). Covered in `test/tank-share.test.js`.
- `src/main/ipc/tank.js`: `tank:get`, `tank:save` (which also answers with what he made of it: new pieces, moving day, sets), `tank:life` and `tank:lived` (his favourite and sets on display), `tank:seen`, `tank:share` (on your cards or not) and `tank:peek` (a friend's, from their last fetched card). The editor sends the whole draft on Done, and `tank.sanitize` decides what of it he can really have: unlocked, within his finds, within the tank's room, a size he's grown into.
- `src/main/wardrobe/catalog.js`: the `decor` kind. `src/wardrobe/tank-decor.json` is the built-in pack.
- **Milestones:** `src/wardrobe/milestones.json` is a built-in pack of decor gated on `unlock: { achievement }`, each the one reward of its own entry in `wardrobe/achievements.js` `ACHIEVEMENTS` (the stats come from every game system, `docs/GAMIFICATION-PLAN.md` section 1.4). `test/packs.test.js` checks the pairing both ways and that every system has a piece. `tank.library` puts a piece from the `milestones` pack on the `milestone` shelf (whatever its catalog category) with `milestone: true`, and gives every locked piece `hint` (the trophy's description, from the wardrobe's `lockInfo`) and, for a trophy, `progress: { current, goal }`; `view` passes them to the tray and the placed pieces. `tank-render.js` paints locked milestone tiles as a silhouette (every palette key in its darkest colour, at 35%), with the lock and a conic progress ring (`--ms-fill`), earned ones with a gold corner, and plays `frames` while a tile is hovered or focused (never with motion turned down). `tank.js` turns the trophy toast into **Put it in the tank**. Tank sizes are gated on `d.everLevel()`, so a Molt keeps them. `ipc/tank.js` fires the tank quests (`decorate`, `bigger`, `milestone-piece`, `jar-in-tank`, `tank-share`; an id quests.js doesn't have yet does nothing). Covered in `test/tank.test.js`, and the shelf end to end in `scripts/e2e-wrapped.js`.
- `src/renderer/panel/tank-paint.js`: paints a tank in art pixels at a whole-number scale, for the Tank tab and the Health porthole. `resolve` lays out a draft the same way `tank.view` does.
- `src/renderer/panel/tank.js` and `tank.css`: the Tank tab and the editor. It draws at 10 frames a second only while the tab is showing, and a single still frame when motion is turned down or the panel is calm (behind your windows, away, a game up).
- `src/main/tank/gauges.js` (pure) turns a Health snapshot, his mood, `DevServers#summary()`, `config.lastUsage`, the last merge and the unread cards into coarse readings (`tempOf`, `loadStep`, `moodOf`, `lighthouseOf`, `tideOf`, `chestOf`, `bottleOf`, `gauges`); `src/main/ipc/tank-gauges.js` keeps the latest, hears the monitor's `sample`, the mood, the dev servers' `change`, each usage reading (`wiring/sessions.js`), a merge (`wiring/github.js`, with a timer to end the glint) and a recap or weekly card going out (`away-service.js`, `wiring/progress.js`), and pushes `tank:gauges` only when the reading differs and the panel is showing. `tank:gauges` asks once, `tank:live` turns one off (`config.tankLive`, per PC), `tank:bottle-read` says the recap was dismissed (`recap.js`) or the week seen (the Trophies page). `src/renderer/panel/tank-gauges.js` paints the reading into placed `glass-thermometer` pieces, the lighthouse, bubbles, plant sway and moods through `tank-paint.js`'s `gauges` option; `tank-gauges-more.js` paints the tide gauge, the chest's glint and the bottle. Covered in `test/tank-gauges.test.js` and `test/tank-gauges-feed.test.js`.
- `src/main/tank/glass.js`: `withStickers` adds `sticker:<projectId>` pieces (the Sticker Book's small art, one each) to the tank's library. `tank/share.js` and `profile-card.js` keep them off the cards. Covered in `test/tank-glass.test.js`.

Kept apart from `config.tank`, so the calling card can't reach it. `src/main/ipc/tank-layouts.js` answers `tank:layouts` (and looks at the seasons then), `tank:layout-save`, `tank:layout-use`, `tank:layout-remove`, `tank:layout-season` and `tank:layout-undo` (one step, kept in main; refused once the tank or the list has moved on). Covered in `test/tank-layouts.test.js` and `test/tank-layouts-undo.test.js`.
- `src/main/tank/tidy.js` (pure, `config.tankTidy`, per PC): `pick`, `apply` and `undo`; `src/main/ipc/tank-tidy.js` answers `tank:tidy` when the tab opens, `tank:tidy-undo` and `tank:tidy-set`. Covered in `test/tank-tidy.test.js`.
- **End-to-end check:** `node scripts/e2e-tank-gauges.js` fakes a hot PC and checks the thermometer and the water, turns a gauge off, saves and puts up a layout (and a season's, when one is running), turns tidying off, and restarts to see it all kept. `node scripts/e2e-tank.js` decorates with the keyboard, checks what main refuses, looks through the Health porthole and restarts to see it all kept. `node scripts/e2e-friends.js` shares it on the calling card, peeks at a friend's (hostile bits and all) and earns House Guest; `node scripts/e2e-card.js` checks the crab card paints it.
