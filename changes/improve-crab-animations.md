### New
- **He works the way Claude does.** While Claude reads or searches he squints at a little scroll, while it edits he scribbles with a pencil, a command has him turning a wrench, the web gets a spyglass, a plan a checklist, and handing work to helpers gets a wave. Switching tools, he ducks into his shell for the next one. Sessions outside Shellby count too, and the OBS browser source shows it all.
- **He moves between moods instead of snapping.** A crouch and a spring before work, a perk-up when a question comes in, a nod when you answer, and a big contented breath after a win (or a shake-it-off after an error).
- **Nodding off and waking up take a moment.** His eyes droop, he catches himself once, then he tucks in from the legs up. Waking, his eyes peek out first, blink twice, and the rest of him follows with a stretch.
- **A question you leave waiting gets more insistent, politely.** After 20 seconds he taps his claw now and then; after a minute, a little hop between sways.

### Changed
- **His idle looks less like a loop.** Blinks come at uneven times (one of them double), and every so often he glances one way, then the other. His shadow shrinks as he jumps and spreads as he lands.

### Faster
- **Half the frames while he works.** His scuttle, his helpers and the "..." in his bubble now step together, so a busy crab draws six frames a second instead of twelve, and fewer still while Claude reads.
