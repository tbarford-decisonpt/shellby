### New
- **Copies sort out their clashes together.** When several copies clash with your branch at once, "Ask him to sort it out" lines them up. Each one takes in your branch only after the one ahead of it is home, so they don't clash with each other all over again. Once one is sorted out, Shellby brings it home by itself and the next one starts.
- **Bring home as many as you like at once.** Bringing a copy home while another is still merging or pushing waits its turn instead of tripping over it. You don't have to wait for one to finish before starting the next.

### Changed
- **Bring all home carries on past a clash.** A copy that clashes stays in its copy and the rest still land. The toast then offers to sort the clashing ones out in turn.
