### New
- **GitLab merge requests and pipelines.** Turn on **Settings → GitLab → Watch my merge requests** and they join the inbox, his CI sign and each project's Health card, next to your pull requests. A red pipeline makes him worry, a fixed one makes him dance, a review request makes him raise a claw. **Fix this build** reads the failing job's trace (following a failed child pipeline), **Address the review** quotes the threads still open, and **Ask why** and **Read it with Claude** work too. Everything goes through the glab CLI with the sign-in it already has, so Shellby never sees a GitLab token. gitlab.com and any GitLab your clones point at are found by themselves, and you can add other self-managed hosts.
- **Releases check GitLab pipelines.** For a project whose clone points at GitLab, the Releases card says whether the pipeline passed on the commit you'd release, and links to its tags.
