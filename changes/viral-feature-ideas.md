### New
- **Tide events.** Six times a year, for a week or so, something's going on at sea: Harvest Moon, The Haunting, Frostbite, Pen Pal Week, Spring Clean and Low Tide. Each has a banner with a countdown on the Us page, four goals counted from what you already do, a bug of its own for the Bugdex, two finds that only turn up while it's on, and a twist (twice the digging at Low Tide, half again in XP at Harvest Moon). Finish every goal while it's on for that year's medal, the event's trophy and a piece for his tank. The trophy and the piece come back next year; a year's medal never does. Switch: **Settings → Safety nets → Tide events**.
- **Event bugs.** Each tide event's bug only comes along with a real fix made its way: the Will-o'-Wisp with a fix after 9 pm at The Haunting, the Lovebug when a helper crab was in the fight in Pen Pal Week, the Dust Bunny with a fix that deletes more than it adds in Spring Clean. Caught ones stay in the book for good; missed ones say when they're back.
- **Sparkly finds.** Now and then a find comes up sparkly: the same find in colours you'll rarely see (1 in 128, more at Low Tide). Shiny bugs, which the Bugdex has had all along, get the same moment now: the panel stops, a card flips over to it, big, with the odds and how many came before it, and **📸 Share this** makes a picture of it. Two new trophies, *Glimmer* and *Shiny Hunter*, for a Glitter Jar and a Sparkle Trail, and a secret one.
- **Crab eggs.** From level 5, with Visiting crabs on, he can lay an egg a week for someone who doesn't have Shellby yet. Send them the invite (the download link and a code); they install, paste the code on the Us page, and it hatches into a baby crab you both keep, and you're friends. A baby can follow him round the desk. Trophies: *Proud Parent*, *Big Clutch* and, for the one who hatched it, *Hatchling*, with a Baby Bib.
- **Swaps with friends.** Trade a spare find with a friend, sparklies too: **Swap…** on their row in Visiting crabs shows what they'd swap (and which of your sets each would finish) and what you could give. Your copy is set aside until they answer, and comes back if they don't within a week. You always keep one of everything. Trophies: *Fair Trade* and *Missing Piece*.
- **The friends' board.** With **Share my Bugdex with friends** on, the Bugdex page ranks you and your friends by the bugs Claude fixed for each of you this month, with a tab for each habitat and one for sparklies, and **📸 Share the board**. Top it for a month for *Top Crab*.
- **Sign Shellby's bring-home commits.** An opt-in line at the end of the commits Shellby makes bringing a copy home: `Shipped-with: Shellby (Lv 12 Abyssal Admin)`. Claude Code's own commits keep their own sign-off. **Settings → Folder**.

### Changed
- **The pull request badge says more:** his title and class, the bugs Claude fixed for you this week, and the tide event's emoji while one's on.
- **Your calling card** now carries what you'd swap, your open eggs (as a code nobody can hatch from the card alone) and your tide event medals, which show on your row in friends' lists.
