### New
- **Notes.** A place for ideas before they're tasks, one list for each project and a General one, on the bar (Ctrl+8). Each note has **Plan** (Claude plans it and nothing changes until you approve), **Build** (it goes to Claude as written) and **Ask** (Claude says whether it's a good idea, changing nothing), and remembers the conversation it started.
- **Conversations side by side.** **Split** (Ctrl+\\) puts another conversation next to the one you're in, or drag a tab into the chat: near an edge splits it, anywhere else swaps it in. Up to four at once. Drag a tab out of the panel, or press **Open in its own window**, and it gets a window of its own.
- **Claude knows it's in Shellby.** It can have him say something, celebrate a real milestone, put on a hat or check his status, and it's told when your usage passes 80% so it can keep things lean. When it fits what you asked, it can offer a Shellby feature (a routine, focus time, a look over your changes, phone alerts) as a card you tap or ignore. Switches: **Settings → Claude**.
- **See an edit before you allow it.** A permission card for a file change shows it in red and green with the file's own line numbers, and every edit row folds open to the same diff.
- **File links.** Paths in a conversation open in VS Code, Cursor or Windsurf at that line. **Settings → Editor** picks which.
- **Find and zoom.** Ctrl+F finds text in a conversation, and Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- / Ctrl+0 size the panel's text, which stays that way.
- **Your friends and settings follow you.** With Sync on, your friends list and your settings (never Autonomous) reach your other PCs too.

### Changed
- **The usage meter keeps up by itself.** It checks when he starts, when you open the panel and when the PC wakes, so usage from your terminal or another PC shows without sending a message. The check costs nothing.
- **Esc never hides the panel.** It closes a menu or goes back a screen, and stops there. The hotkey and × still hide it.
