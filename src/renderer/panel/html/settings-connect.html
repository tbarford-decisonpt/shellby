Tell me when I'm away

Shellby can send a line to your phone when he needs permission, when a long task finishes, when your usage limit resets, or when something is overheating. Usually it's one scan of a QR code: no account, and nothing goes through a server of ours.

  1. Install ntfy on your phone (free, Android or iPhone).
  2. Point your camera at the code and open the link, or add the topic in the app by hand.
  3. Press Send a test below.
