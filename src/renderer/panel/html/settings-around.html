Each of these is off until you turn it on, and says what it reads and where anything goes.

What he notices

Listening along

Shellby notices what's playing on this PC — Spotify, a browser tab, anything that shows up in the volume flyout — and puts his headphones on. Read from Windows itself: no account, and nothing leaves the PC.

Typing along

While you type, in any app, he taps along on a little keyboard, bobs to keep up when you're quick, and watches a long fast burst with stars in his eyes. He hears that a key was pressed, never which one, and none of it leaves the PC.

The weather outside

Pick your town and he dresses for the real weather: a sou'wester and umbrella in the rain, a bobble hat in the snow, shades on a hot afternoon. Every 30 minutes Shellby asks Open-Meteo (free, no account) about your town's rough position, to within about 11 km. It also tells him which half of the world you're in, so spring comes when yours does.

Where he shows up

Desk lighting

Put his mood on your keyboard and fans through OpenRGB: coral while he works, amber when he needs you, red when a build goes red or something overheats. Shellby can install it for you and starts it in the tray whenever the lighting is on.

    On your Discord profile

    Show his level and what he's up to under your name on Discord, the way a game does: Lv 12 Abyssal Admin · working with Claude Code. Shellby talks to the Discord app on this PC, so there's nothing to sign in to, and your friends list sees it while Shellby and Discord are both open.

    Never in Work mode. With visiting crabs on, there's a button to his calling card too.

    On a stream

    Serve Shellby as a browser source, on a transparent background, so he can sit in the corner of a stream and react while you work. It's the same crab, in the same outfit, on 127.0.0.1 only.

    —

    In OBS: + → Browser, paste that URL, and tick Shutdown source when not visible.

    Stream Deck

    Hardware keys for Allow, Deny, Stop, Bring it home and Ready to review, each showing what's waiting. Like answering from your phone, a question, a plan or anything the card would warn about says Look instead, and opens here. Needs Stream Deck 7.1 or newer.

    Stream Deck asks before it installs them. Then drag the keys you want from Shellby in its list of actions.
