This runs the project's own scripts on this PC: its test script, and a typecheck or build if it has one. He asks once per project before the first run, and keeps sign-in tokens and keys out of their environment. The verdict is stamped on the turn's changes. It also checks a copy before bringing it home, and won't merge red without asking. Off by default: tests use your CPU. "Run checks" on any turn works either way.

Now and then, when one turn takes failing tests all the way to green, or a copy comes home with its checks green on the first try, he makes a little fuss. About

When Shellby crashes or closes unexpectedly, a report helps get it fixed. It has the error, versions and the log's last lines, with your home folder and anything token-shaped removed, and goes to Sentry. Nothing is sent without your say-so.
